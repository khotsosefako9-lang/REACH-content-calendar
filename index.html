01 Why this structure

The goal is a feed people keep watching.

These are the working assumptions behind everything in this document.

  • 01
    Fixed days, flexible formatsEach pillar has a set day. What gets posted on that day can change from week to week.
  • 02
    Three rest days a weekTuesday, Thursday and Saturday stay quiet. A page that posts daily gets scrolled past.
  • 03
    Every post has a way to respondA caption question, a poll, or a prompt to tag someone. Built in, not left to chance.
  • 04
    Each pillar points somewhereMinistry spotlights, bible study content and devotionals lead people toward Sunday.
  • 05
    The audience is everyoneKids' parents, students, seniors, first-time visitors, long-time members. One feed, different reasons to stop scrolling.
  • 06
    Built for the current setupPhone, CapCut, Canva. Nothing here requires new equipment.

05 Pillar 02: Bible study

Same day, different format each week.

Wednesday carries the post. Thursday's second session stays off the feed, which keeps the rest days intact. Rotating between these formats keeps the Wednesday post from repeating itself.

Recap videoVideo

A 30 to 60 second cut of the session, worship and discussion included.

Passage breakdownCarousel

The verse studied that week, explained slide by slide.

Quote cardSingle image

One line pulled from that night's discussion or teaching.

Question of the weekReel or graphic

A discussion question from the session, reopened for people to answer in the comments.

08 Events and carousels

Events use an existing day.

Instead of adding a posting day, event content takes the nearest pillar's slot, with a format that changes from event to event.

Countdown carousel3 slides

A short countdown in the week before: date, what to expect, why it matters.

Behind the planningReel

A short clip of the team setting up, posted ahead of the event.

The posterStatic

One consistent poster format for the core announcement.

The recapVideo or carousel

Posted in the following week's spotlight or devotional slot.

09 Platforms

Same content, tuned per platform.

Instagram and Facebook run the same posts on the same days, adjusted for how each is actually used.

Instagram

  • Feed carries the polished version: carousels, shorter captions, reels for anything with movement
  • Stories reshare each feed post, plus extra behind-the-scenes clips
  • First line of the caption needs to hold attention before "more" gets tapped

Facebook

  • Same visuals, longer captions, this audience reads and shares more
  • Events feature used for anything with a date, with its own reminders
  • Recap videos uploaded natively here too, older and extended family are more likely to be on Facebook

11 Roles and workflow

How the week gets produced.

Built around the current setup: two people, phone, CapCut, Canva.

Saturday

Shoot day. Film the ministry spotlight: an interview plus a few shots of the ministry in action.

Sunday to Monday

Edit and post. Cut the spotlight in CapCut, build the carousel in Canva, post Monday morning.

Ongoing

Bible study and Sunday. Captured live at each session and service, edited the same week.

12 Open questions

A few decisions worth making as a team.

This is a starting structure, and these are the calls worth making before the first month runs.

  • Does Thursday's second bible study ever get content?Or does it stay fully off the feed, to protect the rest days.
  • How much short vertical video beyond the two recaps?Worth deciding if spotlight or devotional posts move more toward video over time.
  • Who signs off before something goes live?One approver keeps tone and quality consistent across a two-person team.
  • One event poster template, or room to vary by event size?A conference or outreach may call for different treatment than a small youth event.
